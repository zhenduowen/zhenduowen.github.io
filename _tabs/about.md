I am currently a third-year PhD candidate at the Department of Information Engineering in CUHK, where I have the privilege to be advised by [Prof. Amin Gohari](https://www.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/faculty/gohari-amin/). Prior to this, I earned my master's degree from the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC San Diego, where I worked with Prof. Alon Orlitsky. I graduated from CUHK-Shenzhen with first-class honors in 2022.

My research interests lie at the intersection of theory and application, spanning information theory, learning theory, estimation, and data-driven methodologies. On the theoretical side, I am interested in understanding the fundamental properties of complex systems through mathematical characterizations, asymptotic analysis, and impossibility results. On the engineering side, I focus on problem-driven approaches involving system dynamics, optimization, algorithm and architecture design, computational efficiency, and probabilistic methods that capture patterns not explicitly represented by existing models.

I appreciate the complementary roles of mathematical reasoning and empirical investigation in advancing scientific understanding, as reflected in the following observation:

> “The development of Western science is based on two great achievements: the invention of the formal logical system in Euclidean geometry by the Greek philosophers, and the discovery of the possibility of finding causal relationships through systematic experiment during the Renaissance.”
>
> — Albert Einstein (1953)
